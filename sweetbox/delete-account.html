Delete Your Account and Data

Sweet Box: Candy Dots & Boxes · Developer: ASAP Studios (Bhavesh Kumar), India

You can ask us to delete your Sweet Box account and all the data linked to it at any time. You do not need to have the app installed to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to bhaveshkunagvanshi@gmail.com with the subject "Sweet Box data deletion request".
  2. Send it from the Google account email you used to sign in to Sweet Box. If you played without Google Sign-In, include your in-game display name and the approximate date you last played online.
  3. Tell us whether you want your whole account deleted or only certain data (for instance, only your statistics).
  4. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you. We then delete your data within 30 days and email you when it is done.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (Google-linked or anonymous user ID).
  • Your online profile: display name, profile photo URL and Google account email and ID as held by Sweet Box.
  • Your online game statistics (games played, games won, boxes captured, win rate).
  • Any remaining online room, presence or chat data linked to your user ID.

What may be kept

  • Records of in-app purchases are kept by Google Play under its own policies, and by us only where the law requires (for instance tax or accounting records).
  • Your deletion request email, kept only as long as needed to show we completed it.

Multiplayer rooms are already deleted automatically when each game ends, so there is normally no match history left to remove.

Delete data on your device

Your coins, gems, boards, campaign progress and settings are stored only on your phone. To delete them, uninstall Sweet Box, or open Android Settings > Apps > Sweet Box > Storage > Clear data. This cannot be undone.

Remove Google access

You can also disconnect Sweet Box from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. This stops future sign-ins but does not by itself delete data we hold, so please also send us a deletion request.
