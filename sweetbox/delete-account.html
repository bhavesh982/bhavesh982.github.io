Delete Your Account and Data

Sweet Box: Candy Dots & Boxes · Developer: ASAP Studios (Bhavesh Kumar), India

You can ask us to delete your Sweet Box account and all data linked to it at any time. You do not need the app installed to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us
    Write to bhaveshkunagvanshi@gmail.com with the subject "Sweet Box data deletion request".
  2. Identify your account
    Send it from the Google account email you used in Sweet Box. If you never signed in with Google, include your in-game display name and when you last played online.
  3. We delete it
    We may reply to confirm the request is yours, then delete your data within 30 days and email you when it is done.

Email a deletion request

You can also ask us to delete only some data (for instance, only your statistics) instead of your whole account.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (Google-linked or anonymous user ID).
  • Your online profile: display name, profile photo URL, and the Google account email and ID held for Sweet Box.
  • Your online game statistics (games played, games won, boxes captured, win rate).
  • Any remaining online room, presence or chat data linked to your user ID.

What may be kept

  • Records of in-app purchases are kept by Google Play under its own policies, and by us only where the law requires, such as tax or accounting records.
  • Your deletion request email, kept only as long as needed to show we completed it.

Multiplayer rooms are already deleted automatically when each game ends, so there is normally no match history to remove.

Delete data on your device

Your coins, gems, boards, campaign progress and settings are stored only on your phone. To delete them, uninstall Sweet Box, or open Android Settings > Apps > Sweet Box > Storage > Clear data. This cannot be undone.

Remove Google access

You can disconnect Sweet Box from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. This stops future sign-ins but does not by itself delete data we hold, so please also send us a deletion request.

For more details, read our Privacy Policy.
