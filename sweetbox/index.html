Sweet Box

Candy Dots & Boxes

🍬

Connect the dots, close the boxes, win the candy!

Sweet Box puts a candy-coated twist on the classic Dots and Boxes game you played on paper. Take turns drawing lines, close a box to claim it and take another turn. Whoever owns the most boxes when the board is full wins.
