Privacy Policy

Sweet Box: Candy Dots & Boxes (Android package com.asapstudios.sweetbox)
Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sweet Box mobile game ("Sweet Box", "the app") collects, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. Sweet Box is developed and published by ASAP Studios, operated by Bhavesh Kumar, India ("ASAP Studios", "we", "us", "our"). By installing or using Sweet Box you acknowledge this policy.

In short: You can play Sweet Box offline without an account. Your coins, gems, boards and campaign progress are stored on your device. We only process account and multiplayer data when you choose to sign in with Google or play online. Sweet Box does not currently show ads, we do not sell your data, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

ASAP Studios (Bhavesh Kumar), India, is responsible for the personal data processed through Sweet Box. Contact: bhaveshkunagvanshi@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect and why

2.1 Information stored only on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved locally on your device: coins and gems balance, unlocked boards, campaign levels and stars, selected board size and difficulty, your in-game display name, and sound, music and vibration settings. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

2.2 Anonymous player ID (Firebase Anonymous Authentication)

When you use online features, the app signs you in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This creates a random user ID that is not linked to your name or email. It is used to identify you as a player in online rooms and to protect rooms from unauthorised changes.

2.3 Google Sign-In (optional)

You may choose to sign in with your Google account to secure your profile. If you do, Google shares your Google account name, email address, profile photo URL and Google account ID with the app through Firebase Authentication. We use this to create and secure your Sweet Box account, show your name and photo on your profile and in online matches, and keep your online profile across devices. Signing in with Google is never required to play.

2.4 Online profile and statistics

If you are signed in and use online play, we store a player profile linked to your user ID: display name, profile photo URL, and game statistics such as games played, games won, total boxes captured and win rate. This lets the app show your profile and stats.

2.5 Online multiplayer

When you create or join an online room, the following is processed through Firebase Realtime Database: the room code, your player ID, display name and colour, the moves made and the scores, the game state, your online/offline status while in a room, and any chat messages you send in a match. This information is visible to the other players in the same room and is needed to run the match.

2.6 In-app purchases

Sweet Box may offer optional purchases of in-game coins and gems. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive only the information needed to deliver the item (such as the product purchased and a purchase confirmation). We never receive or store your card number or other payment details.

2.7 Technical information

Like any internet service, Google Firebase and Google Play services automatically process basic technical information when the app connects to them, such as your IP address, device and operating system type, app version and timestamps, to provide, secure and keep these services reliable.

What we do not collect

Sweet Box does not access your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location, and it does not include analytics or advertising tracking tools.

3. Advertising

Sweet Box does not currently show any ads and does not use an advertising ID. If we add ads in a future version (through Google AdMob), we will update this Privacy Policy before that version is released, explain what data the ad provider processes, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the game, including saving your progress and running online matches.
  • To create, secure and manage your optional account.
  • To show your profile, display name and statistics.
  • To deliver in-app purchases.
  • To prevent cheating, abuse and unauthorised access, and to fix problems.
  • To respond to your messages and requests, including deletion requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We process this information because it is needed to provide the features you choose to use, with your consent (for instance when you sign in with Google), for our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure and working, or to comply with the law.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. Information is shared only:

  • With service providers that run parts of the app on our behalf:
    • Google Play services and Google Sign-In: Google Privacy Policy
    • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions): Privacy and Security in Firebase
    • Google Play Billing: Google Privacy Policy and Google Payments Privacy Notice
  • With other players, limited to your display name, profile photo, colour, moves, scores and chat messages in rooms you join.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our players, ourselves or others.

6. Data retention

  • Multiplayer rooms: rooms and their moves are deleted when the game ends or when the host leaves or disconnects. Your online/offline status is cleared when you leave.
  • Account, profile and statistics: kept while your account exists, and deleted within 30 days after you ask us to delete them.
  • On-device data: kept on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Deleting your data

  • Account and online data: follow the steps on our Delete Your Account and Data page, or email bhaveshkunagvanshi@gmail.com with the subject "Sweet Box data deletion request". We will delete your Firebase account, profile and statistics within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • On-device data: uninstall Sweet Box, or go to Android Settings > Apps > Sweet Box > Storage > Clear data.
  • Google access: you can also remove Sweet Box's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

8. Children

Sweet Box is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data sent between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to online data is restricted by authentication and database security rules, so players can only change rooms they belong to. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. International data transfers

Firebase and Google Play services are operated by Google, which may store and process data on servers in India, the United States and other countries. Google applies safeguards for these transfers as described in its privacy and security documentation.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, withdraw consent, and object to or restrict certain processing. This includes the rights of a Data Principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including grievance redressal and nominating another person to exercise your rights. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR, including data portability and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. California residents have rights under the CCPA to know, delete and correct their personal information; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, email bhaveshkunagvanshi@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

12. Grievances

For any privacy concern or complaint, contact our grievance contact, Bhavesh Kumar, at bhaveshkunagvanshi@gmail.com. We aim to resolve grievances within 30 days.
