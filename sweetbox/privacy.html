Sweet Box: Dots & Boxes - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how ASAP Studios ("we") handles information in the Sweet Box mobile game (package com.asapstudios.sweetbox).

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email, contacts, location, or any account details. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, unlocked items and settings) is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalise ads. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Sweet Box is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because your progress is stored on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. For ad data, use your device's advertising ID controls or contact Google.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
